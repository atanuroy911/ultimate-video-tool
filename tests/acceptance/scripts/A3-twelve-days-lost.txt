Day one. Mara missed the last bus and walked into the forest to find a shortcut. Day two. The path she knew was gone, and her phone had no signal. She decided to follow the stream downhill. Day four. She ran out of water bottles but found a spring and drank from it. Day six. It rained all night, and she built a shelter from branches and leaves. Day nine. She heard a distant engine and shouted until her voice cracked. Day twelve. A farmer found her sitting by the river, tired but alive. She never takes shortcuts anymore.
