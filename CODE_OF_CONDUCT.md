# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short summary. The full text applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in their second or third language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give and take feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use the tool.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [atanuroy911](https://github.com/atanuroy911) on GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may warn, suspend or ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
