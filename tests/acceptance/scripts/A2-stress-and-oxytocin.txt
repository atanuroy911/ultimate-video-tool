Why does a hug make stress melt away? It starts in your brain. When someone holds you, your body releases a hormone called oxytocin. Oxytocin tells your heart to slow down. It tells your muscles to loosen. Meanwhile the stress hormone, cortisol, begins to fall. Within about twenty seconds, your breathing gets deeper. That is why a long hug works better than a quick one. So the next time you feel tense, find someone you trust and hold on a little longer.
