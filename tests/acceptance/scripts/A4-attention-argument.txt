Attention is not a resource you have. It is a currency you spend. Every notification is a bill. Every scroll is a purchase. Economists call this the attention economy, and its products are designed to make you overspend. The cost is not time, it is focus. Focus is what you pay for deep work, for good conversation, for sleep. So the question is not how to find more time. The question is what you are buying with the attention you already have.
