On a cold island lived an old lighthouse keeper named Tomas and his grey cat, Pepper. Every night Tomas climbed three hundred steps to light the lamp. Every night Pepper followed him and sat by the window. One stormy night the lamp went dark. Tomas was ill in bed and could not climb. Pepper ran up the stairs alone, knocked the lantern, and rang the old brass bell until the harbour boats heard it. The ships turned safely away from the rocks. In the morning the whole village brought fish for the cat.
