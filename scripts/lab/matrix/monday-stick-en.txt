It is Monday morning, and the alarm rings. You hit snooze once. Then twice. Then you jump out of bed in a panic because it is already eight. You brush your teeth while putting on one sock. You run to the bus stop and watch the bus drive away. You sit on the curb, sigh, and open your coffee. It is cold. Then your friend drives by and offers you a ride. Sometimes Monday is rough, and sometimes it is kind.
