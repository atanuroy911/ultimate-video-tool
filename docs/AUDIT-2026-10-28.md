## Update, the acceptance run (all ten)
Passed the judges: **A1, A4, A5, A8**. Failed: **A2** (words per picture 69 %), **A3** (length 53.6 s against 40 s; words per picture 60 %), **A6** and **A7** (the two Bangla videos are 19.3 s and 16.3 s against 35 s and 28 s; words per picture), **A9** (words per picture 75 % of 4 pictures), **A10** (143.6 s against 210 s). Two causes, neither fixed by loosening a limit:
* **Words per picture** fails on 7 of 10: the slots often carry fewer or more words than the 85 % rule wants. That is a real finding about the slot rules (`vt/reel/slots.py`), not about the judge.
* **Length** fails on the Bangla videos and A10: the targets in `tests/acceptance/corpus.json` imply 96 to 114 words a minute, far slower than the voices speak (Bangla 174), so these targets were guesses. Whether to change them is [Q22](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md); A3 and A10 are the other way round.
A2 and A3 were first run without the server settings and failed at the voice step; the runner now copies the owner's engines (never a key) into its scratch Studio.

## What this means
The voice, picture, reel, judge and engine layers work and are tested. The two structural pieces the plans build everything else on are still missing: **the direction set with its evaluation** (Phase 4 gates, Phase 5) and **the plan review before Start** (Phase 6). Most remaining "built" items fail only on proof: a real run, a second machine, or a person watching.
