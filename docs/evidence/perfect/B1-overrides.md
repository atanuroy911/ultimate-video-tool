## What is not an override
- The rule director's own plans (`rules`): the guard and the clearing stay for them.
- Captions: chunking, highlight words and reveal come from the words and the theme; a scene's `caption.y` is the director's and is honoured.
- Anything on a scene the person edited by hand (`edited`, free-placed elements): every rule above skips it.

B2 and B3 (renderer and composer, one flag on the scene: `directed`), B4 and B5 and B6 (the validators, with their notes), B7 (one test that composes a crowded plan for every style and every kind of director), then B8 to B11.
