Meet Ultimate Video Tool: the free, open-source studio that turns your voice into a finished video.
Record yourself in English, Bangla or Banglish. Or just type a script, and a natural voice reads it for you.
It cleans the audio, cuts the silences and the ums, and times every single word.
Then an AI director plans every scene. It picks pictures that match your words, designs a look, and adds captions, music and sound effects.
Numbers turn into charts. Steps turn into checklists. Stories turn into cartoons that act them out.
Want a change? Tell the director in plain words, or move any element yourself on the timeline.
Direct it with a local AI model, or with Claude or Codex through MCP. No API key needed.
Run it all on one computer, or keep the heavy work on a graphics server and edit from any laptop.
