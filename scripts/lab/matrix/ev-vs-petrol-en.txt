Electric car or petrol car? Let us compare. Buying price: electric cars cost about ten thousand dollars more today. Fuel: charging at home costs about a third of petrol per kilometre. Service: electric cars have fewer moving parts, so yearly service is cheaper. Range: petrol wins on long trips, with five hundred kilometres against about three hundred and fifty. Resale: still uncertain for electric. If you drive more than fifteen thousand kilometres a year, the electric car usually wins within five years.
