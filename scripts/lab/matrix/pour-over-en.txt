Here is how to brew a good cup of pour over coffee in four steps. Step one, weigh fifteen grams of coffee and grind it medium, like coarse sand. Step two, heat water to ninety-six degrees, just off the boil. Step three, pour a little water to wet the grounds and wait thirty seconds. This is called the bloom. Step four, pour slowly in circles until you reach two hundred and fifty grams. The whole brew should finish in about three minutes. Taste it, and adjust the grind next time.
