Why do leaves change colour in autumn? All summer, leaves are green because of a helper called chlorophyll. It catches sunlight and makes food for the tree. When days get shorter, the tree stops making chlorophyll. The green fades away. Now you can see the yellow and orange that were hiding there all along. Red leaves are different. The tree makes new red colour from sugar stuck in the leaf. So the next time you see a red leaf, remember, it is a tree's last painting before winter.
