Your smartwatch battery is not dying because it is old. It is dying because of five small settings. First, the always-on display. It redraws the screen every second and can eat a third of your battery. Second, constant heart rate checks. Every minute is more than most people need. Third, notifications from apps you never read. Each one wakes the screen. Fourth, a bright screen outdoors. Fifth, a weak phone connection, which makes the watch search all day. Change these five and most watches last two extra days.
